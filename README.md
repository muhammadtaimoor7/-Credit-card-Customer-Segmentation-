# Credit Card Customer Segmentation

This project uses K-Means Clustering to segment credit card customers based on their financial behavior, without relying on any predefined labels. Features like Balance, Purchases, Credit Limit, and Payments were selected and scaled, and the Elbow Method was used to determine the optimal number of clusters, which came out to four.

The clustering revealed four distinct customer segments: premium high spenders with the highest purchases and payments, low activity customers with minimal engagement, moderate spenders with growth potential, and high balance customers who hold funds without spending much. These insights help the business design targeted strategies such as premium rewards for high value customers and onboarding incentives for low activity users.

## Tech Stack
Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Seaborn, Matplotlib
